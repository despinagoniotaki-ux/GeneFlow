Effective date: 20 April 2026
Owner: Bioanalytical Operations
1. Purpose
Define the standard process for preparation, analysis and recording of study samples.
2. Applicable assay
For GT-101 biomarker analysis, analysts shall use AM-001 version 1.0 unless an approved change control
explicitly states otherwise.
3. Sample input
The standard sample input volume for AM-001 is 10 µL.
4. Records
Analysts shall record method ID, method version, sample ID, run ID and any deviations from the applicable
method.
5. Change control
Changes to the analytical method require documented review and approval before implementation.
