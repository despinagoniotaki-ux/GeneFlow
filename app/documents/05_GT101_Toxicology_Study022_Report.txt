GT-101 Toxicology Study 022 — Final Report
Fictional controlled-use document | Prepared for GeneFlow demonstration

Study ID: GT101-022
Report version: 1.0
Study completion: 10 June 2026
1. Objective
Evaluate tolerability, clinical pathology, histopathology and selected immunogenicity endpoints following
administration of GT-101.
2. Analytical methods
Immunogenicity samples were analysed using AM-001. The study laboratory record identifies AM-001 version
1.0 and a 10 µL sample input.
3. Findings
No unexpected clinical findings were identified at the proposed development dose. Histopathology findings were
reviewed in the context of dose and control groups.
4. Interpretation
The immunogenicity results contribute to the overall preclinical evidence package. Analytical method version
should be considered when comparing these data with future studies.
5. Conclusion
The findings support continued nonclinical development, subject to integrated review of the complete evidence
package.
