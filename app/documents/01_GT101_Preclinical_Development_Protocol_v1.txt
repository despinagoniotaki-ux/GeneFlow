GT-101 Preclinical Development Protocol v1.0
Fictional controlled-use document | Prepared for GeneFlow demonstration

Version: 1.0
Status: Approved for preclinical execution
Date: 12 March 2026
Candidate: GT-101
1. Objective
Evaluate preliminary efficacy, biodistribution and tolerability of GT-101 in a relevant disease-model study.
2. Study design
Four dose groups will be evaluated. The planned total enrolment is 40 animals, with 10 animals per group.
Primary analyses will use animals meeting the protocol-defined analysis criteria.
3. Key endpoints
Primary: target-gene expression and disease-relevant biomarker response.
Secondary: biodistribution, clinical observations and selected immunogenicity measures.
4. Analytical method
Samples designated for biomarker analysis will be analysed using AM-001, version 1.0. The planned sample
input volume is 10 µL.
5. Sample schedule
Samples will be collected at baseline and at predefined post-dose time points. Analytical results will be linked to
animal ID, dose group and collection time.
6. Data handling
Any deviations from the planned sample size, analytical method or sampling schedule shall be documented and
assessed before final interpretation.
