GT-101 Preclinical Efficacy Study 014 — Final Report

Study ID: GT101-014
Report version: 1.0
Study completion: 28 May 2026
1. Study objective
The study evaluated preliminary pharmacodynamic activity, biodistribution and tolerability following
administration of GT-101.
2. Study population
Forty animals were enrolled across four dose groups. Thirty-eight animals were included in the final efficacy
analysis after protocol-defined exclusions and sample-quality review.
3. Analytical procedures
Biomarker samples were analysed using AM-001. The laboratory record identifies the method as AM-001
version 1.0. Sample input volume was 10 µL.
4. Results
Dose-related increases in the primary biomarker were observed in the evaluable population. Biodistribution was
detected in the target tissue in all active dose groups.
5. Deviations
Two animals were excluded from the primary efficacy analysis because of predefined analysis criteria. No
amendment to the study protocol was issued.
6. Conclusion
The study provides preliminary evidence of pharmacodynamic activity and supports continued evaluation of
GT-101. Interpretation of historical analytical results should take account of the method version used.
