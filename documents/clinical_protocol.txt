Section: Study Overview
Content: Phase I/II open-label dose-escalation study of rAAV-hFIX gene therapy in adults with severe Haemophilia B.
Sample Size: n=40 participants enrolled across four dose cohorts.

Section: Vector Description
Content: The investigational product is rAAV-hFIX (single-stranded recombinant AAV) encoding codon-optimised human Factor IX.
Assay Reference: Potency measured using AM-001 v1 (deprecated analytical method).

Section: Dose Escalation
Content: Starting dose for Cohort 1 is 1.5e12 vg/kg, escalating to 3.0e12 vg/kg in Cohort 2.
Safety Reference: Preclinical NOAEL established in NHP toxicology study (see preclinical_toxicology.txt).

Section: SOP References
Content: Sample handling per SOP-302 v2. Data management per SOP-101 v4.
