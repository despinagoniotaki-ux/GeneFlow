Section: Scope
Content: This SOP governs the validated use of potency assay AM-001 v2 as the approved current method.

Section: Supersession Notice
Content: AM-001 v1 is formally retired as of 2024-03-01. Any document referencing AM-001 v1 must be updated to AM-001 v2.

Section: Validation Parameters
Content: AM-001 v2 demonstrated linearity (R2=0.998), precision (%CV<5%), and accuracy (95–105%) across the validated range.
