Document: Preclinical Toxicology Report
ID: TOX-PRE-003
Version: 1.2
Date: 2023-06-10
Author: Dr. B. Nguyen

Section: Study Design
Content: GLP-compliant repeat-dose toxicology study in non-human primates (NHPs). Vector: scAAV-hFIX (self-complementary AAV).

Section: NOAEL
Content: The No-Observed-Adverse-Effect Level (NOAEL) was determined to be 5.0e12 vg/kg based on histopathology and liver enzyme data.

Section: Safety Limit for FIH Translation
Content: The maximum recommended starting dose (MRSD) for first-in-human (FIH) translation is 1.5e11 vg/kg, applying a 1/10 safety factor from NOAEL.

Section: Vector Note
Content: All preclinical studies conducted with scAAV-hFIX (self-complementary). The FIH product must confirm vector equivalence before clinical translation.
