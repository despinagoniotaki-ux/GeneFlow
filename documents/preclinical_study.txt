Document: Preclinical Efficacy Study
ID: PRE-EFF-002
Version: 1.0
Date: 2023-04-20
Author: Dr. C. Williams

Section: Vector
Content: scAAV-hFIX (self-complementary AAV serotype 8) expressing codon-optimised hFIX-Padua variant.

Section: Efficacy Endpoints
Content: FIX activity reached 85% of normal at week 12 post-administration at 1.0e12 vg/kg in NHPs.

Section: Conclusion
Content: scAAV-hFIX demonstrated durable Factor IX expression with acceptable safety profile. Supports FIH dose selection.
